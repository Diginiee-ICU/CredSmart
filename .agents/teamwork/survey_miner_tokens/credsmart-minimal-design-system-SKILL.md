# Copy of credsmart-minimal-design-system SKILL.md
(Source: .agents/skills/credsmart-minimal-design-system/SKILL.md)

Ultra-high production-grade master design standard for CredSmart by CredAlert.
Defines full-stack minimalist UI/UX tokens, typography hierarchy, neutral surface color architecture,
fast-access desktop productivity layouts, and native-feeling touch-first mobile ergonomics.
Enforces 100% desktop-mobile divergence with zero regression across business workflows.

Core tokens:
- Primary Sans Font: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif
- Monospace & Tabular Numbers: 'IBM Plex Mono', ui-monospace, monospace with font-mono tabular-nums
- Hierarchy: 24-30px (hero), 18-20px (page titles), 14-15px (card/body), 12-13px (tables/metadata), 11px (badges/shortcuts). No sub-11px micro-fonts.
- Light Canvas: #F8FAFC (bg-slate-50), #FFFFFF (cards), #E2E8F0 (border-slate-200/80)
- Dark Canvas: #090D16 (bg-slate-950), #111726 (bg-slate-900), #1E293B (border-slate-800)
- Accent: Royal/Electric Blue #2563EB (light) / #3B82F6 (dark)
- Semantic: Emerald #10B981, Rose #E11D48, Amber #F59E0B
- Button standard: 34px (h-8.5 px-3 rounded-lg text-xs font-semibold shadow-2xs), 28px (h-7 px-2.5 rounded-md text-[11px] font-medium), 30px (w-7.5 h-7.5 rounded-md), 46px (mobile center elevated CTA). Zero bloated h-11, h-12, rounded-3xl bubble buttons.
- Elimination: heavy 3D glass reflections, skeuomorphic bevels, liquid glass, glowing neon halos.
